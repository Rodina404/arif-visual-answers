The researchers in this paper tested chain-of-thought prompting on a large number of models and benchmarks to make sure the result was not a coincidence, so on the model side they used five families, the first being GPT-3 in its InstructGPT versions with sizes of 350M, 1.3B, 6.7B and 175B, the second LaMDA with sizes of 422M, 2B, 8B, 68B and 137B, the third PaLM with sizes of 8B, 62B and 540B, the fourth UL2 at 20B, and the fifth Codex, which is code-davinci-002, and on the benchmark side they divided them into three types, the first being arithmetic word problems with GSM8K, SVAMP, ASDiv, AQuA and MAWPS, the second commonsense reasoning with CSQA, StrategyQA, Date Understanding, Sports Understanding and SayCan, which is about turning an instruction for a robot into a sequence of actions, and the third symbolic reasoning with a task where the model concatenates the last letter of each word in a name and a task where a coin is flipped or not flipped and the model has to say whether it is still heads up.
